Organization	Role
Kenya Electricity Generating Company (KenGen) -	Generates most of Kenya's electricity.
Independent Power Producers (IPPs) - Private companies that also generate electricity.
Kenya Electricity Transmission Company (KETRACO) -	Builds and operates high-voltage transmission lines.
Kenya Power (KPLC)- Distributes electricity to customers.
Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority (EPRA) - Regulates the energy sector and publishes reports and statistics.

How has Kenya's generation mix changed over time?
Which generation sources contribute most to meeting demand?
